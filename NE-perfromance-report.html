In Table 1 there are 5 columns. For your understanding, the columns are described below-

  1. Month-Year: This column states the month and year of sales.

  2. Customer Reached: This column states the number of customers reached in a month.

  3. New Customers: This column states number of new customers reached who did not buy MMS before. There might be a confusion between column 2 and columns 3. For your understanding, column 2 means in a specific month how many unique customers reached, on the other hand, column 3 means in the same month how many of the unique customers are new (first time buyer)

  4. Repeat Customers: It simply a calculation (column 2-column 3), means they are repeatedly buying MMS

  5. Retention Rate (%): This is the customer retention rate for MMS by month.

From Table 1, 331 unique customers were reached up to September in Shantiganj Upazila. The most noticeable thing is the trend of reaching new customers which is decreasing over month. During developing DIP we targeted 10 new customers per month per NE but in the data the number is significantly lower.

5. Conclusion

It seems that the progress has a lower trend of reaching the pregnant women through MMS where we have other project partners (CARE, THP). In this cases, we can concentrate on the project activities such as cooking demo, mother campaign, seeds selling, etc.

The other upazilas where iDE is implementing solely, we need to identify the low performed NEs and provide the need based program support to reach more pregnant women. Though the sales has a increasing trend in these upazilas, we need to be cautious about any upcoming risks which might affect the sales.
